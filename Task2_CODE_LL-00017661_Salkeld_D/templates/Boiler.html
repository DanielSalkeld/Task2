Modern Day boilers

  1. Installing Modern boilers can offer efficiency levels of over 92% (depending on the make and model) and also use condensing technology. This means less fuel is wasted, and more energy is converted into heat.
  2. A new system boiler will cost between £1,600 to £5,000 to install. On average, you can expect to pay around £3,300. A system boiler is the perfect choice for homes with a higher hot water demand – this could mean 2 or more bathrooms, or large families who need to use multiple outlets at once.

During work you can!

  1. We can aim to reduce paper use, replace single-use plastics and try to recycle as much as possible. We can also choose efficient appliances or improve our buildings with better insulation.
  2. • To run your offices or larger operations like warehouses through renewable energy could mean installing solar panels or an air source heat pump. You can provide cheap, clean electricity and heating for your business through these.
  3. • Car-sharing to work is a great way to reduce emissions from your own and colleagues.
  4. • Avoid single-use coffee cups, glasses, silverware, and plates.
  5. • Promote a paperless culture.
  6. • Promote waste recycling.
  7. • Choose efficient office lighting.

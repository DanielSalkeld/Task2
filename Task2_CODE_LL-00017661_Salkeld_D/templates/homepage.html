• What is a carbon footprint? - Any person or business can have a carbon footprint. This is a calculated total of how much carbon you emit in your daily activities. This can include, for example, how you choose to travel, which foods you eat and how you use energy throughout your home. Each aspect of your routine can directly or indirectly contribute to your carbon emission footprint.
